1. Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is a famous historical monument located in Agra. It is known for its beautiful architecture.

2. India Gate

India Gate is a famous monument located in New Delhi. It is an important landmark of India.

3. Golden Temple

The Golden Temple is a famous religious and historical place located in Amritsar, Punjab.

4. Red Fort

The Red Fort is a historic fort located in Delhi. It is an important monument of Indian history.

5. Gateway of India

The Gateway of India is a famous monument located in Mumbai. It is a popular tourist attraction.
